FAQ

Common questions

Is it safe to connect my bank account? +
Yes. We use Plaid — the same technology used by Venmo, Coinbase, and thousands of other apps. We get read-only access and never see your login credentials.
Which banks are supported? +
Over 12,000 US financial institutions including Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citi, and most credit unions.
Is Arkonomy really free? +
The core features are completely free. We offer a Pro plan for advanced AI insights, investing features, and unlimited account connections.
How is this different from Mint or YNAB? +
Arkonomy uses real AI (powered by Claude) to give you specific, actionable insights — not generic tips. It understands your actual patterns and tells you exactly what to change.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. Arkonomy is a Progressive Web App — install it on your iPhone or Android from the browser. No app store needed.
What happens to my data? +
Your financial data is encrypted, never sold, and used only to generate your personal insights. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

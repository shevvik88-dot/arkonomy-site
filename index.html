Security & privacy

Designed around controlled access.

Clear boundaries for connected accounts and the information you choose to bring into Arkonomy.

◈

Read-only bank access

Plaid handles bank authentication. Arkonomy does not receive your bank username, password, or MFA code.

⌁

Protected connections

Connections use HTTPS/TLS, while integration secrets remain in server-side functions.

⊘

No data selling

Financial data is used to provide the product experience and is not sold.
